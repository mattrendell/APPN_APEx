DPIRD GOBI flights at York calibration trial (DPIRD project: 2026_CalibrationTrial_I_SIF_DPIRD, DPIRD-internal site name "York_F"; YCD = York Calibration Dpird).

Five flights. All data collected with DPIRD's GOBI sensor mounted on UWA's M350 drone; data collected by Franco (UWA's own GOBI data for the same day collected by the DPIRD team). Along-rows flights were attempted three times because of the GNSS error in UWA's GOBI sensor and the clouds.

run_00 (YCD_Row1)   - along-rows attempt 1; small patch of cloud -> discard, re-flown
run_01 (YCD_Row2)   - along-rows attempt 2
run_02 (YCD_Row3)   - along-rows attempt 3; VNIR ortho initially 4x lower res (6cm/px), reprocessed with run_01's DSM in GPT -> full-res output
run_03 (YCD_Range2) - along-ranges; VNIR lens cap not removed -> discard
run_04 (YCD_EW3)    - East-West direction

run_02 is the SIF Cali flight and pairs with a UWA GOBI flight taken ~5 minutes apart (sensor-consistency comparison); UWA half to be delivered by Warin Chotirosniramit.
